These four numbers drive every status bucket on the dashboard. The command-line tool reads the same values by default — pass a CLI flag for a one-off override without changing this.
A SKU is flagged low stock once its days-of-cover drops below this.
A SKU is flagged overstock once its days-of-cover exceeds this.
A SKU is flagged dead stock once this many days have passed with no sales since its last purchase (or since it was first tracked, if it's never been purchased).
How many days of recent sales history "days of cover" is averaged over. A wider window smooths out one unusually strong or weak month; a narrower one reacts faster to recent changes.
